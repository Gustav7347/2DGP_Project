# UI icons and original frame artwork

Icons made by **Lorc**, available on [Game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net), licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Original author notice: `../licenses/game-icons-license.txt`. Individual source SVGs are preserved in `source-icons/` and upstream blob IDs in `sources.json`.

Used icons: bloody-sword, moon, crossed-swords, blade-bite, crossed-slashes, bleeding-wound, eye-shield, ankh, potion-ball, ninja-mask, mountaintop, pointy-sword, run.

Modifications: remove the solid square background, use a white mask for SDL runtime tinting, rasterize to 128px PNG. The shapes themselves are preserved. Icons are credited in the in-game F1 help and this distributed file. No endorsement by the original creator is implied.

The iron/ink frames, seals, vignette and straw-hat portrait `wanderer` are original code-drawn SVG artwork for this project, generated by `tools/build_ui.cjs`; they are not taken from Kenney or Game-icons.net. Their SVG sources and PNG exports are both included. No AI raster image editing was used to export the vector UI.

Authoring requires Node, Playwright and Chrome. The game loads only the PNGs and does not depend on that authoring toolchain.
